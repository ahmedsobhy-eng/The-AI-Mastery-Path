# Module 01: What is Data Science?

## Overview
In this module, I explored the foundational definitions of Data Science and what makes a successful Data Scientist.

## Key Takeaways
- **Definition:** Data Science is the process of uncovering hidden patterns and insights from data to solve complex problems.
- **The Data Scientist's Role:** It's not just about math and code; it's about being curious, telling a story with data, and helping organizations make informed decisions.
- **Big Data & Mining:** Learned how massive datasets are structured and the importance of "mining" them for gold (insights).

## Goal
To build a solid conceptual foundation before diving into technical tools and programming.

Note: This module is focused on foundational concepts and theoretical frameworks. No code implementation was required.

"I realized that Data Science is more about curiosity than just raw coding"
