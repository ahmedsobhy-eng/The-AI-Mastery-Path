# 🤖 Module 02: Deep Learning and Machine Learning
## 📈 Focus: Regression Analysis

### 🔍 Overview
In this module, I explored the foundations of **Supervised Machine Learning**, specifically focusing on **Regression**. This technique is the cornerstone for predicting continuous numerical values.

### 🧠 Key Concepts
* **Definition:** Regression is a statistical method used to estimate the relationship between a dependent variable (outcome) and one or more independent variables (features).
* **Historical Origin:** First studied by **Sir Francis Galton (1886)**, who discovered that offspring traits tend to "regress" toward the average.
* **Supervised Learning:** Learning from historical data to predict future outcomes.

### 🏠 Real-World Application: Real Estate
I analyzed a study of **500,000 property sales** to understand the determinants of housing prices:
1.  **Infrastructure:** Proximity to subways and highways.
2.  **Location:** Distance from the downtown core.
3.  **Features:** Number of washrooms, lot size, and exterior materials (Brick vs. Stone).

### ❓ Why Regress?
Regression helps answer critical business questions like:
- "How much value does an additional bedroom add to a house?"
- "What is the impact of nearby high-voltage power lines on property value?"

### 🧠 Deep Learning & Generative AI Takeaways
- **Neural Networks (NN):** The computational building blocks inspired by the human brain.
- **Deep Learning (DL):** An advanced form of NN with **multiple layers**, capable of learning from massive unstructured data.
- **Generative AI:** A subset of DL focused on **creating new content** (Text, Images, Code) rather than just classifying existing data.
- **Real-world Application:** **Recommender Systems** use machine learning to predict user preferences based on historical behavior (e.g., Netflix/YouTube suggestions).
